            Frequently Asked Questions

            Do I need to know Hindi or Punjabi?

            English can be useful for academic and university communication. Learning basic Hindi and Punjabi phrases may help you communicate in everyday situations.

            Where can I get help with visa-related concerns?

            Contact the university's international student support service and consult official Indian government immigration resources. Requirements depend on your circumstances.

            What currency is used in India?

            India uses the Indian Rupee (INR), represented by the symbol ₹. Visit Banking Assistance for further guidance.

            How can I get a local SIM card?

            Visit an authorized mobile network provider and confirm identification, age eligibility and documentation requirements. Ask your guardian or university support service for assistance where necessary.

            How can I find suitable food on campus?

            Explore the Food and Dining section and ask dining staff about ingredients, dietary requirements and food allergies.

            What should I do if I feel homesick?

            Stay connected with your family, participate in suitable campus activities and speak with a trusted adult, mentor or university counselling professional when needed.

            What should I do during an emergency?

            Call 112 for immediate police, fire or medical emergencies in India. You can also approach nearby university security personnel or a trusted staff member.

                Frequently Asked Questions

                Find answers to common student banking concerns.

                How can I find banks near LPU?

                Use the Find Banks option in Quick Access or check the official branch locator of your preferred bank. Confirm the location and opening hours before visiting.

                What documents are required to open a bank account?

                Requirements depend on the bank, account type, age and residency status. Use the document checklist as general preparation and confirm the actual requirements with your bank.

                Can students under 18 have a bank account?

                Banks may offer accounts for minors, subject to applicable eligibility and operating conditions. Consult your bank and parent or guardian for the appropriate arrangements.

                What should I do if a UPI payment fails?

                Check the transaction status in your official payment application. If money has been deducted, avoid repeating the payment until its status is confirmed. Contact your bank or authorized payment service for assistance.

                What should I do if an ATM does not dispense cash?

                Check whether your account was debited. Keep the transaction reference and contact your bank through its official customer support channels.

                How can international students get banking assistance?

                International students should contact an authorized bank and the university's international student support services to understand applicable documentation, eligibility and currency requirements.

                Where can I exchange foreign currency?

                Consult an authorized bank or authorized foreign exchange provider. Confirm applicable identification requirements, exchange rates and fees.

                What should I do if someone asks for my banking OTP or PIN?

                Do not share it. End suspicious communications and contact your bank using verified official contact information.

                How can I report online financial fraud?

                Inform your bank immediately. In India, suspected financial cybercrime can also be reported through the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or the official cybercrime portal.
